      Supported social & website links

      Choose a name to view its links. We recognize the platforms below, plus any other HTTP or HTTPS website with a globe icon. Icons use platform symbols or initials.

      Use one record per line: Full Name, Alternate Full Name, https://x.com/example, https://instagram.com/example, https://onlyfans.com/example. Each comma separates a complete field; keep every full-name variation intact as one field. A bare @handle needs a platform label. For platforms marked “Full profile URL,” paste the complete address, including the profile path. Links are supplied by contributors and are not verified accounts.

      Each URL belongs to one entertainer. The importer rejects links already assigned to another name, including equivalent Twitter/X links. Duplicate links within a name are combined.
